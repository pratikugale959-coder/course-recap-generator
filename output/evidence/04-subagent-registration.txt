NOTE (free-tier limitation discovered during development): file-based
agents carrying `deny` permission rules fail on the OpenCode Console
free tier with "Error from provider (Console): OpenCode's free tier can
only be used from within OpenCode". The shipped configuration therefore
uses allow rules only; read-only enforcement lives in the agent's system
prompt. See output/evidence/06-reviewer-session.txt for the successful
launch produced by this configuration.
